How to Use This Tool

Quick Start

  1. Pick your market type — Standard for preferred carriers, Non-standard for specialty/high-risk
  2. Set minimum scores — Slide to filter out low-quality areas
  3. Click ZIP codes — See detailed demographics, competition, traffic, and crashes

What the Scores Mean

Overall Opportunity (0–100%)
One number combining competition, traffic, safety, and demographics. Higher = better place to open an office or run ads.
Standard Market Fit (0–100%)
High home values, lots of vehicles, franchise agents (Geico, State Farm, Allstate). Best for preferred carriers.
Non-Standard Market Fit (0–100%)
High renter %, lower income, independent agents, Spanish-speaking. Best for specialty/high-risk carriers.
